1. What the App does

Wi-Note lets you keep your Wi-Fi network details (network name, password, security type) and personal notes as cards on your device, and share your Wi-Fi details as QR codes. Main features:

  • Generating and displaying QR codes for Wi-Fi networks, including full-screen view
  • Storing note, password/PIN and emergency cards (with categories, tags, icons and colors)
  • Reading a Wi-Fi QR code from an image in your gallery
  • Scanning nearby Wi-Fi networks (Android requires the location permission for this)
  • Exporting saved Wi-Fi networks to a CSV file
  • Optional biometric / screen-lock verification
  • Theme and language options

The App does not require an account, registration or sign-in.
